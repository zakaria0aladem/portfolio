<br>Autonomous Sumo Combat Robot<br>

An autonomous combat robot developed for Sumo-style robotics competitions, where two robots compete to push each other out of the ring. The robot combines sensor-based opponent detection with fast motor control and strategic movement algorithms for competitive performance. We participated in the competition three times, refining the design with each iteration from improving mechanical stability and traction to optimizing control logic and decision-making speed. Each version strengthened the robot’s performance and provided hands-on experience in electronics, embedded programming, and team-based engineering.
