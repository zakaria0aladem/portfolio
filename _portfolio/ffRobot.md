<br>Firefighting and Rescue Robot – IEEE Competition Project <br>

An autonomous firefighting robot featuring a servo-actuated robotic arm, differential drive, and a vacuum-based suction system designed to capture and hold ping-pong balls as target objects.<br> The system combined mechanical design, embedded control, and actuator coordination to enable precise object pickup and transport. This project focused on integrating motion control with manipulation under competition constraints.
