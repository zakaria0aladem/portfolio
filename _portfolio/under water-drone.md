Developed a remotely operated 5-degree-of-freedom underwater drone equipped with five brushless thrusters, a Raspberry Pi–Pixhawk control system running ArduSub, tethered Ethernet communication, QGroundControl integration, and real-time video streaming with AI-assisted hazard detection. I designed and assembled the waterproof electronics pipe that housed all electronics, created its schematic, and integrated the ESCs, sensors, and communication protocols to ensure stable underwater operation and reliable data transmission.
