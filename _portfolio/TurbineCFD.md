## Overview<br>
Designed and simulated a specialized wind turbine assembly to evaluate aerodynamic performance and torque generation. The project utilized Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to validate the design’s effectiveness in converting kinetic wind energy into mechanical power.<br>
<br>
## Design & Modeling<br>
<br>
### Mechanical Design<br>
- Unique vertical/tilted axis blade geometry designed for high-torque capture at variable wind speeds.<br>
- 3D assembly modeled in SolidWorks, featuring a reinforced central shaft and optimized blade curvature to maximize drag-based propulsion.<br>
- Integrated support structure designed for stability under high aerodynamic loads.<br>
<br>
### Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)<br>
- Conducted using SolidWorks Flow Simulation to analyze the interaction between the turbine and air currents.<br>
<br>
#### Simulation Parameters<br>
- External flow analysis within a virtual wind tunnel environment.<br>
- Real-time tracking of velocity vectors and pressure distribution across blade surfaces.<br>
- Convergence monitoring using defined simulation goals to ensure numerical accuracy.<br>
<br>
## Results & Analysis<br>
<br>
### Performance Metrics<br>
- Simulation achieved convergence after 69 iterations, providing stable and reliable performance data.<br>
